# 90-Day Income Generation Plan

**Goal:** $1,000 income | **Budget:** $100 | **Timeline:** 90 days

---

## Strategy: Multi-Stream Digital Income

Allocate the $100 budget across 3 parallel income streams to minimize risk and maximize return.

### Stream 1: Freelance Services ($0 startup) — Target: $500
**Platform:** Fiverr, Upwork, LinkedIn

| Week | Action | Revenue Target |
|------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | Set up profiles, create 3-5 gig listings (writing, data entry, virtual assistant, web scraping, spreadsheet work) | $0 |
| 2-3 | Deliver first orders at low price ($5-15) to build reviews | $50 |
| 4-6 | Raise prices, apply to Upwork jobs daily | $150 |
| 7-10 | Repeat clients + referrals, upsell services | $200 |
| 11-13 | Maintain momentum | $100 |

**Key gigs to offer (no special skills needed):**
- Data entry / spreadsheet formatting
- PDF to Word/Excel conversion
- Social media post scheduling
- Basic research & summarization
- Resume formatting

### Stream 2: Print-on-Demand ($30 budget) — Target: $250
**Platform:** Redbubble, TeePublic, Merch by Amazon

| Week | Action | Revenue Target |
|------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | Sign up for accounts, research trending niches | $0 |
| 2-3 | Create 30 designs using Canva (free) + upload | $0 |
| 3-4 | Spend $30 on social media promotion (Pinterest + Reddit) | $30 |
| 5-8 | Upload 20 more designs, optimize listings with SEO | $80 |
| 9-13 | Scale winners, remove losers, hit 100+ designs | $140 |

**Winning niches:** Niche hobbies, professions, pet breeds, funny quotes, seasonal themes.

### Stream 3: Digital Products ($70 budget) — Target: $350
**Platform:** Gumroad, Etsy, Notion Marketplace

| Week | Action | Revenue Target |
|------|--------|---------------|
| 1-2 | Create 3 digital products (templates, checklists, planners) | $0 |
| 3 | List on Gumroad + Etsy ($15 Etsy listing fees) | $0 |
| 4-5 | Spend $55 on targeted promotion (Etsy ads, social media) | $50 |
| 6-9 | Create 3 more products based on what sells | $150 |
| 10-13 | Optimize, bundle products, scale ads on winners | $150 |

**Product ideas:**
- Budget tracker spreadsheet (Google Sheets/Excel)
- Social media content calendar template
- Notion productivity dashboard
- Wedding/event planning checklist
- Small business invoice template

## Daily Action Checklist

### Morning (30 min)
- [ ] Check all platform notifications & messages
- [ ] Respond to client inquiries within 1 hour
- [ ] Apply to 3 Upwork jobs (if freelancing)

### Afternoon (1-2 hours)
- [ ] Complete active freelance orders
- [ ] Create 2 new POD designs OR 1 digital product component
- [ ] Engage in 2 relevant subreddits/communities

### Evening (30 min)
- [ ] Update revenue tracker
- [ ] Schedule next day's social media posts
- [ ] Research trending topics/keywords for tomorrow

---

## Risk Mitigation

1. **If freelancing is slow:** Double down on digital products (zero marginal cost)
2. **If POD doesn't sell:** Reallocate ad budget to Etsy digital products
3. **If one stream hits:** Focus 70% energy there, maintain others passively
4. **Always:** Track ROI weekly, kill what doesn't work, scale what does
