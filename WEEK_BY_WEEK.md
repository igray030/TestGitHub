# Week-by-Week Action Plan

## PHASE 1: SETUP (Week 1)
**Spend: $0 | Earn: $0**

- [ ] **Day 1-2: Freelance Setup**
  - Create Fiverr account, set up 5 gigs (data entry, PDF conversion, spreadsheet work, research, resume formatting)
  - Create Upwork profile with portfolio samples
  - Set prices LOW to get first reviews ($5-10 per gig)

- [ ] **Day 3-4: Print-on-Demand Setup**
  - Sign up for Redbubble + TeePublic (free)
  - Sign up for Canva free account
  - Research 10 profitable niches on Etsy/Redbubble bestsellers

- [ ] **Day 5-7: Digital Products Setup**
  - Create Gumroad account (free)
  - Create Etsy shop ($0.20/listing)
  - Build first product: Budget Tracker Spreadsheet (Google Sheets)
  - Build second product: Social Media Content Calendar

## PHASE 2: LAUNCH (Weeks 2-4)
**Spend: $30 | Target Earn: $90**

### Week 2
- [ ] List first 2 digital products on Gumroad + Etsy
- [ ] Upload 15 POD designs to Redbubble
- [ ] Apply to 5 Upwork jobs daily
- [ ] Complete first Fiverr orders (even at $5 each)

### Week 3
- [ ] Create 15 more POD designs (total: 30)
- [ ] Spend $15 promoting POD on Pinterest
- [ ] Build 3rd digital product: Notion Productivity Dashboard
- [ ] Get first Fiverr reviews, raise prices slightly

### Week 4
- [ ] Spend $15 on Etsy ads for digital products
- [ ] Analyze which POD designs get views, make more in winning niches
- [ ] Apply to higher-paying Upwork jobs ($25-50)
- [ ] Ask satisfied clients for reviews

## PHASE 3: GROW (Weeks 5-8)
**Spend: $55 | Target Earn: $350**

### Week 5-6
- [ ] Raise freelance prices 50-100%
- [ ] Create product bundles on Gumroad
- [ ] Upload 20 more POD designs (total: 50)
- [ ] Spend $25 on best-performing Etsy ads
- [ ] Create 2 more digital products based on what sells

### Week 7-8
- [ ] Focus on repeat freelance clients
- [ ] Spend $30 on social media promotion
- [ ] Optimize all listings with better keywords/SEO
- [ ] Upload 20 more POD designs (total: 70)
- [ ] Create a product bundle/premium tier

## PHASE 4: SCALE (Weeks 9-13)
**Spend: $15 | Target Earn: $560**

### Week 9-10
- [ ] Double down on highest-earning stream
- [ ] Upload remaining POD designs to hit 100+
- [ ] Upsell existing clients on larger projects
- [ ] Launch 1 premium digital product ($15-25 price point)

### Week 11-12
- [ ] Maintain all 3 streams
- [ ] Optimize ad spend — kill losers, scale winners
- [ ] Reach out to past freelance clients for repeat work
- [ ] Cross-promote products across platforms

### Week 13 (Final Push)
- [ ] Final promotion push on best sellers
- [ ] Offer limited-time bundles/discounts to hit goal
- [ ] Calculate final numbers and plan next 90 days

## Key Metrics to Track Weekly

1. **Revenue by stream** — Which stream is earning most?
2. **ROI on ad spend** — Are ads paying for themselves?
3. **Time per dollar** — Which activities pay best per hour?
4. **Conversion rate** — Views to sales ratio on each platform
5. **Repeat customer rate** — Especially for freelancing
