## PROPOSAL TIPS

1. **Always read the full job post** — reference specific details they mentioned
2. **Answer their screening questions** — never skip these
3. **Keep it under 150 words** — clients skim
4. **Lead with value** — what you'll DO for them, not your life story
5. **Apply within first 24 hours** — early applicants get more visibility
6. **Start low, raise later** — your first 5 reviews matter more than early income
7. **Apply to 5-10 jobs daily** — it's a numbers game early on
8. **Follow up after delivery** — ask for a 5-star review politely
